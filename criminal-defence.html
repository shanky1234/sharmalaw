Practice Area 02

Criminal Defence

Calm, confidential, and responsive support when legal proceedings require prompt attention and careful preparation.

Clear support in a serious moment.

Criminal matters may involve urgent decisions and procedures. The chamber takes time to understand the available information, explain the process, and help prepare an appropriate response based on the facts of the case.

Initial case assessment

A focused discussion of the known facts, available documents, and immediate concerns.

Procedural guidance

Plain-language explanation of relevant steps, hearings, notices, and documentation.

Representation support

Preparation and representation in accordance with the case requirements and applicable procedure.

Rights-focused advice

Guidance centred on due process, confidentiality, and understanding available options.

Prepare for the consultation

  • Bring copies of any FIR, notice, summons, bail order, or related documents.
  • Write a simple factual timeline before the appointment.
  • Do not conceal details - complete information helps your counsel advise responsibly.

  • Clear procedural explanation
  • Fact and document review
  • Responsive case preparation
