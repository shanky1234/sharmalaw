Reference schedule

The calculator rounds a fraction of a lakh upward only where the stated rule uses “or part thereof”. A statutory pre-deposit, jurisdiction, valuation, limitation and Registry payment method are outside this calculator.

Rule 7

DRT Original Application

For an Original Application under Section 19, the fee depends on the amount of debt due / claim amount used for filing.

AmountFee used by the calculator
Up to ₹10 lakh₹12,000
Above ₹10 lakh₹12,000 plus ₹1,000 for every additional ₹1 lakh or part thereof, subject to a maximum of ₹1,50,000.
Official Tribunal Rule 7 source ↗

Rule 8

DRAT Appeal

For an appeal under Section 20, the calculator uses these filing-fee slabs based on the relevant debt / appeal amount.

AmountFee used by the calculator
Below ₹10 lakh₹12,000
₹10 lakh to below ₹30 lakh₹20,000
₹30 lakh and above₹30,000
Official Tribunal Rule 8 source ↗

Rule 13

SARFAESI application or appeal

The fee varies based on applicant category and the amount of debt due. The same schedule also applies to an appeal under Section 18 as specified by the rule.

Applicant / amountFee used by the calculator
Borrower: below ₹10 lakh₹500 for every ₹1 lakh or part thereof
Borrower: ₹10 lakh and above₹5,000 plus ₹250 for every ₹1 lakh or part thereof above ₹10 lakh; maximum ₹1,00,000.
Other aggrieved party: below ₹10 lakh₹125 for every ₹1 lakh or part thereof
Other aggrieved party: ₹10 lakh and above₹1,250 plus ₹125 for every ₹1 lakh or part thereof above ₹10 lakh; maximum ₹50,000.
Published Rule 13 source ↗
